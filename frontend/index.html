What we do not promise

No un-downloading

Revoking stops future access. It cannot make someone forget a saved copy.

Audit anchor

A database superuser could rewrite the whole chain unless it is anchored elsewhere.

Small files

Files live in the database, capped at 5 MB each.

FAQ

Common questions

Can admins read my files?

No. Admins manage users and see activity, not contents.

What happens when I revoke access?

The person's next request is refused and the refusal is logged.

Can the audit log be changed?

The app cannot update or delete it, and every row is hash-chained.

Which files can I upload?

PDF, PNG, JPG, DOCX and TXT up to 5 MB, checked by content.
